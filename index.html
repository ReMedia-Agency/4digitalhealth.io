How It Works

A clear, proven path from first contact to your next chapter.

  • 1

    Submit Your Intake Form

    Tell us about your background, goals, and where you are in your journey.

  • 2

    Free Discovery Call

    A 30-minute conversation to understand your situation and confirm we're the right fit.

  • 3

    Personalized Roadmap

    We build a custom coaching plan with milestones, timelines, and clear deliverables.

  • 4

    Weekly Coaching Sessions

    Regular 1-on-1 meetings to work through applications, materials, and strategy.

  • 5

    Review & Submit

    We review everything before it goes out — essays, resumes, applications, and more.

  • 6

    Decision & Beyond

    We help you evaluate options, negotiate offers, and plan your next move confidently.
